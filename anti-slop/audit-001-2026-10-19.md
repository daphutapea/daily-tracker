# antislop audit 001: Daily Tracker v1.5 (19 Oct 2026)

Mode: after (session override). Direction: DESIGN.md. Dials: ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2.
Skills used: core, antislop-ui, antislop-human, antislop-layoutmobile, antislop-copywriting,
antislop-code, emil-design-eng.

How this was checked: read every page and shared component in `src/`, ran the app against a local
Supabase stack seeded with overdue tasks, three tasks due at the default 17:00, two events and two
quick links, then measured it in Chromium at 1280 px and at 390 px with touch. Every text pair in
DESIGN.md was recomputed with the WCAG formula and all of them pass (lowest: `--line-strong` on
`--surface-2` in Navy, 3.10, which is a border and only needs 3:1). No horizontal overflow on any
page at 390 px, no console errors, no em dashes in UI text.

The 15 Oct gate still holds for most of the app. What follows is what it missed or what changed
since.

Priority follows the rule tier: Hard Gate = HIGH, Purpose-Gate = MEDIUM, Quality Locks = LOW.
Findings that only break a Craftsmanship principle (C-1 to C-5) have no tier, so they are ranked by
how often you would hit them.

Nothing is changed until you pick numbers.

## HIGH

**1. Toasts cover the phone tab bar and run off the bottom of the screen.** R-03.
Measured at 390 x 844: the "Moved 2 tasks to tomorrow" toast ends at y = 854, below the screen, and
covers the whole tab bar (which starts at 813) for its 8 seconds. Undo toasts are the main feedback
for deletes and moves, so this happens often. Fix: give Sonner a bottom offset of the tab bar height
plus the safe area while the tab bar is showing (below 768 px), in `src/App.tsx`.

**2. Deadlines at the same time collapse into one mark on the day ruler.** R-26, R-03.
New tasks default to 17:00, so this is the common case, not an edge case. With three tasks due at
17:00 the ruler draws one diamond, and a click or tap only reaches the top one; the other two can't
be opened from the ruler at all. Each mark is also 20 x 32 px, under the 44 px touch minimum. Fix in
`src/components/DayRuler.tsx`: merge marks that are within a few minutes into one mark with a count
("3 due at 17:00") that opens the Calendar day, and give marks a 44 px hit area on touch.

**3. Settings still talks about Telegram, and its time zone row no longer does anything.** R-38.
"Telegram reminders and the morning summary use this time zone." Telegram was removed in v1.5. The
time zone it shows is also no longer read by anything: the Mac app schedules from its own clock, and
Quick add sends the device's zone. The row, its "Use Asia/..." button and `useTimeZoneSync` in
`AppShell.tsx` all describe a setting with no effect. The doc comment above `DataSection` also still
mentions the bot token. Fix: remove the row, the sync hook and the stale comment. The database column
can stay.

**4. Touch targets under 44 px.** R-03.
Measured at 390 px with touch:

| Control | Size | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Filter chips (Open, Today, Overdue, Done, All) | 28 px tall | Tasks |
| Reminder chips (At deadline, 15 min before...) | 28 px tall | Task and event dialogs |
| Tomorrow, Move all to tomorrow | 28 px tall | Home, Tasks |
| Show table | 20 px tall | Home, Review |
| Calendar, Board, Weekly review, Edit (panel header links) | 20 px tall | Home |
| List / Board, Mode switch, Task / Event toggle | 40 px tall | Tasks, Settings, Quick add |
| Editor toolbar buttons | 40 px | Notes, task notes |

Fix: raise `.chip` and these text buttons to 44 px on coarse pointers (the same way `.btn` already
does), and lift Segmented and the toolbar from 40 to 44.

**5. Quick add saves an event's location without showing it.** R-38.
The AI returns a `location` for events and `toEventRow` saves it, but the draft card has no field for
it. DESIGN.md promises "nothing is saved until you press Add" and that every draft is an editable
card; here part of the AI's reading goes in unseen, and a wrong guess only turns up later on the
event. Fix: add a "Where" field to event drafts in `QuickAdd.tsx`, matching the event dialog.

**6. Quick add accepts an event that ends before it starts.** R-27.
`draftProblem` checks title, day and start time, not the end. "Standup 10 to 9am" saves as an event
ending an hour before it begins. The event dialog already catches this ("The end is before the
start."). Fix: the same check in `draftProblem`.

## MEDIUM

**7. Moving an event's start leaves its end behind.** C-4.
Change a 14:00 to 15:00 event to start at 16:00 and Save fails with "The end is before the start."
Calendars keep the length and move the end with the start. Fix: in `EventDialog.tsx`, when the start
changes, shift the end by the same amount.

**8. Changing a task's deadline leaves its reminder at the old time.** C-4.
Pick "1 hour before", then move the deadline from Friday to Monday: the reminder stays on Friday and
the chip silently unpresses. Fix: when the reminder matches a preset, move it with the deadline.

**9. New note leaves an empty "Untitled note" behind.** C-4.
Home's New note button creates the row before you type. Open it, change your mind, and an empty
"Untitled note" stays at the top of Notes. Fix: when you leave a note that has no title and no text,
delete it quietly (no Undo toast needed).

**10. Small motion and hover details.** R-19. Low impact, ranked by its tier.

| Before | After | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Reduced motion on a phone: the sheet appears and disappears with no transition | Keep a 200 ms opacity fade on phones under `prefers-reduced-motion` | DESIGN.md says reduced motion keeps fades and drops movement; on phones it drops both |
| `hover:bg-surface-2` on Tomorrow and Move all to tomorrow | Use the existing `row-hover` style behind `(hover: hover)` | On touch the tint sticks after a tap |
| `.btn-ghost` has no hover state | Same `--surface-2` hover as `.btn-quiet` | The dialog close button and month arrows give no hover feedback on a computer |

## LOW

**11. Home shows three different counts for "today".** C-3, R-31.
Side by side: "Today 5 left" (includes overdue), "Due today 3" (open only), and "Deadlines met, 1 of 4
done" (includes the finished one). Each is right on its own terms; together they read as a mismatch.
The Numbers row also repeats Overdue, Due today and Events today, which the Today panel right above
already shows. Fix: drop the repeated tiles, keep Open tasks and Done in the last 7 days, and label the
meter "Due today, including done".

**12. Home panels stretch to the tallest neighbour and leave empty blocks.** Part 3 (whitespace as
structure).
At 1280 px, Next 7 days with one item is stretched to the height of Today, and In progress with two
items is stretched to the height of the month, leaving a large blank area in each. Fix: `items-start`
on the two Home grids, so each panel is as tall as its content.

**13. Links asks "Are you sure?" before deleting; everything else offers Undo.** R-31.
DESIGN.md: "Deleting doesn't ask; it deletes and offers Undo for 8 seconds." Tasks, events and notes
do that; quick links use `window.confirm`. Fix: delete straight away and offer Undo with the same
`useOfferUndo` helper.

**14. Copy and labels.** R-15.
- Calendar's header buttons read "Task" and "Add event"; everywhere else it is "Add task".
- Review's panel title "The week after, 27 Oct on" reads oddly. "Week of 27 Oct" says it.
- Board hint says "Drag a card"; on a phone it takes a press and hold first. "Drag a card (press and
  hold on a phone)" says how.

**15. Labels screen readers skip.** C-4.
`aria-label` on the day ruler's wrapper `<div>` and on the clock's `<p>` is ignored, because neither
has a role. Fix: `role="group"` on the ruler and `role="timer"` or a plain visually hidden time on the
clock.

**16. Decorative separator comments.** antislop-code, R-31.
`// ---------------------------------------------------------------- board` style banners in
`QuickAdd.tsx` (4), `Tasks.tsx`, `Settings.tsx`, `queries.ts`, and `/* ---------- Dialog ... ---------- */`
in `index.css` (15 in total). Fix: replace each with a plain one-line comment, or remove it where the
next function name already says it. The `{/* hour ticks */}` comment in `DayRuler.tsx` restates the
code and can go. Comments only; no code changes.

## What holds up

Kept as is, because each has a reason written down and checked: the palette and its contrast in all
four themes, the day ruler motif, the single teal accent, Undo instead of confirm for tasks, events
and notes, the instant keyboard-opened Quick add, focus rings, the skip link, empty, loading and
error states on every data view, and no fabricated numbers or copy anywhere.
